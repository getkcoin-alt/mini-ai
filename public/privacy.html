Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 November 2026 · Mini is operated by [operator name], India. Contact: [contact email].

What Mini is

Mini is an AI voice companion. You talk, Mini listens and talks back. Mini is a computer program, not a person, and not a doctor, therapist or lawyer.

What we store

  • Your account: your email address (or Google account email) used to sign in.
  • Your profile: what Mini calls you, your birth year, and the preferences you choose (friend or mentor, tone, what's on your mind, things to avoid). Only saved after you agree.
  • Memories: short notes about you that you add or approve. You can see and delete each one in the app.
  • Usage: how many minutes you talked each day, to apply fair-use limits.

What we do not store

  • We do not record or keep your voice. Audio is processed live and discarded.
  • We do not keep transcripts of your conversations on our servers. Server logs record only timings and lengths, never what was said.
  • We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Where it is processed

Your account, profile and memories are stored with Supabase (database provider). Live voice is processed on Mini's own private server in India and reached through Cloudflare. The website is hosted on Vercel. These providers process data only to run Mini.

Children

Mini is currently for people aged 18 and over. If you are under 18, the app will not save your details. A version for young people with a parent's consent is planned.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile any time in You; delete memories in Memories.
  • Delete your whole account in Settings → Delete my account. This permanently erases your profile and memories.
  • Questions or requests: [contact email].

Safety

If you tell Mini you might hurt yourself, Mini will share free helpline numbers such as Tele-MANAS 14416. In an emergency call 112.
